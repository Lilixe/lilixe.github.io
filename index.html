Projects

Job Match Dashboard

A full-stack job scraping and matching platform that scrapes job postings from Wanted Korea, extracts required skills from job descriptions, compares them to your own skill set, and ranks jobs by match score. Includes a database-backed application tracker.

Tech Stack: Python, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy, SQLite/PostgreSQL, Streamlit, Requests, BeautifulSoup

  • Scrapes job title, company, URL, and full job description.
  • Extracts skills using keyword pattern detection.
  • Computes a match score (%) between job requirements and user skills.
  • Stores jobs in database and tracks status (new / unfit / applied).
  • Allows resume PDF upload and automatic skill extraction.
